What's inside, and why.

Men's skin is thicker and oilier, and it gets shaved most mornings. So every formula is light, oil-controlling and gentle on freshly shaved skin. Every ingredient is on the pack.

In Prime and Base

Niacinamide

Vitamin B3. Helps regulate oil through the day and, with regular use, evens out tone.

In Prime

Hyaluronic acid

Holds water in the skin so the base never clings to dry patches around the nose or beard line.

In Prime

Kaolin clay

A soft white clay that absorbs excess oil. It's why Prime dries matte, not shiny.

In Lock

Aloe vera

Calms razor irritation and cools on contact. Useful on a 38°C morning.
