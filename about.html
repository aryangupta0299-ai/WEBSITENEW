We made the thing we couldn't find.

Makeup that works on men's skin, matches Indian skin tones and doesn't look like makeup.

It started before a wedding. Swollen eyes after two late nights, a red patch from a bad shave, and a photographer booked for eight hours.

The options were a sister's concealer that turned grey on brown skin, or a salon foundation that looked like a mask by lunch. Neither was made for a face that gets shaved every morning, sweats through a Mumbai afternoon and isn't meant to look made up.

So we spent two years building a routine that is: a primer that controls oil instead of adding it, a skin tint sheer enough to disappear and pigmented enough to hide what you want hidden, and a spray that keeps both in place through Indian summers.

We kept it to three products and three minutes, and made eight shades because Indian skin isn't one colour. We packed it in matte black so it sits on your shelf like it belongs there.

Nobody should notice your makeup. They should notice you.

What we won't compromise on

Invisible by design

If someone can tell you're wearing it, we got it wrong. Every formula is tested on camera, in daylight and under office lights.

Built for Indian skin

Shades developed on Indian skin, across warm, neutral and olive undertones, from wheatish to deep.

Three minutes, maximum

No brushes, no sponges, no tutorials. If it takes longer than your shave, it doesn't make the range.
