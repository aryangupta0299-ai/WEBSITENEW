Common questions

Using Bronam

Will people be able to tell?

Not if you use the right amount. Base is sheer and buildable. Start with one pump for your whole face and blend towards the jaw and hairline so there's no edge.

Does it work with a beard or stubble?

Yes. Apply above the beard line and tap, don't drag, at the edges. Prime and Lock are both fine over stubble.

How do I take it off?

Your normal face wash does it. Wash twice if you've worn all three steps on a long day.

Is it OK for sensitive or acne-prone skin?

All three are non-comedogenic and have no added fragrance. If you have a skin condition, patch test on your jaw for 24 hours first.

Orders, delivery and returns
